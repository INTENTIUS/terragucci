[tacos.guru](https://tacos.guru) publishes a list of 24 features for Terraform automation. This page answers that list for terragucci, with each answer proven by a passing smoke claim on the [validation](/terragucci/reference/validation/) page. It is a checklist, with no score or ranking.

Two blocks carry no claim. The dashed one, RBAC and SSO, stays with your forge, which already decides who signs in and who may approve a merge ([Access and identity](/terragucci/standards/access-and-identity/)). Pricing, outlined, has no claim: terragucci is Apache-2.0, and you pay for your CI minutes and your bucket. Hover or tap a block for its criterion.

## Criteria

The "Asks for" column is tacos.guru's description of the criterion. The terragucci column links the page that shows it and names the claims that prove it on the [validation](/terragucci/reference/validation/) page.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Asks for | terragucci |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CDK Terrain workflow | 5 | synth before plan; plan only the stacks a TypeScript change reaches | [`synth`](/terragucci/guides/plan-cdk-terrain-stacks/) runs before check and plan, and a pull request plans only the stacks it changes. `init/cdktn-synth`, `affected/cdktn` |
| 2 | OpenTofu support | 5 | OpenTofu first-class, its version pinned per stack | OpenTofu is the default binary. A root's `.opentofu-version`, `.terraform-version` or exact `required_version` picks its version, installed in the job and checked against the release's `SHA256SUMS` ([guide](/terragucci/guides/use-a-binary/)). `init/root-pins`, `init/pinned-install` |
| 3 | Self-hosted runners (no K8s required) | 5 | runners in your account; your own images | every stage is a job in your CI, on its runners, hosted or your own, picked by label per stage with [`runner`](/terragucci/guides/add-to-a-repo/#self-hosted-runners), in the published image or one you build on it ([architecture](/terragucci/concepts/how-it-works/), [`image`](/terragucci/guides/run-steps/)). `boot/tofu`, `init/image`, `init/runner-label` |
| 4 | RBAC & SSO | 5 | SAML or OIDC sign-in; roles by team and environment | no accounts and no login: sign-in is your forge's and roles are forge permissions plus cloud IAM. See [Access and identity](/terragucci/standards/access-and-identity/) |
| 5 | Linked-state orchestration | 5 | linked states deployed as one unit: a DAG, real unknowns across states, ordered applies, progress and gates | waves order the applies, each behind its gate; a root plans on its upstream's planned outputs, unknown where unknown, and plans again once the upstream applies; the [run view](/terragucci/concepts/waves-and-approvals/#the-run-view) shows each wave's state ([linked roots](/terragucci/concepts/waves-and-approvals/#linked-roots)). `linked/plan`, `linked/states`, `waves/tofu` |
| 6 | Drift detection | 4 | scheduled, per stack, with remediation | a scheduled run per root opens and closes the drift issue, and turns drift on a literal into a pull request ([guide](/terragucci/guides/turn-on-drift-checks/)). `drift/tofu`, `drift/close`, `respond/drift` |
| 7 | Cloud credentials (OIDC) | 4 | short-lived cloud credentials per stack, no static keys | each job trades the forge's OIDC token for a plan or apply role, per root glob with `oidc.roles`, on AWS, GCP and Azure ([credentials](/terragucci/reference/pipeline/#credentials)). `oidc/forgejo`, `report/oidc`, `oidc/clouds` |
| 8 | Pricing | 4 | the cost to run it | Apache-2.0, with no license fee and nothing hosted; you pay for your CI minutes and your bucket |
| 9 | Repo scaffolding & codegen | 4 | backend, provider and workspace boilerplate from one declarative source | `terragucci generate` writes each root's backend, provider and version files from repo, glob and root settings, and `tf-check` refuses a hand edit ([guide](/terragucci/guides/generate-root-files/)). `generate/tofu` |
| 10 | State governance & RBAC | 4 | state access isolated per environment, fine-grained roles, export, cross-state tracking | a role per environment scoped to its state keys ([guide](/terragucci/guides/scope-state-access/)), an approved and audited [export](/terragucci/guides/export-a-state-version/), [cross-state edges](/terragucci/guides/track-cross-state-edges/), [state versions](/terragucci/guides/find-a-state-version/) and [migrations](/terragucci/guides/move-resources-between-roots/). `oidc/state-roles`, `state/export`, `estate/state-edges`, `state/versions`, `migrate/split` |
| 11 | Migration from Atlantis | 3 | a documented path, `atlantis.yaml` import or comment compatibility | `terragucci import atlantis` and `import digger` write `terragucci.yml`, and `atlantis plan` and `atlantis apply` comments work behind a setting ([guide](/terragucci/guides/coming-from-atlantis-or-opentaco/)). Teams on a hosted platform have guides too: [HCP Terraform, Scalr or OTF](/terragucci/guides/coming-from-hcp-terraform-scalr-or-otf/), [Spacelift or env zero](/terragucci/guides/coming-from-spacelift-or-env-zero/). `import/atlantis`, `comment/atlantis` |
| 12 | Custom workflows, hooks & gates | 3 | any step before or after each stage, custom images, blocking gates | [`ci.hooks`](/terragucci/guides/run-steps/) run before init, before and after the plan (drift's too) and before and after the apply; a failing hook stops the wave or holds it at the gate; `image` runs a root's jobs in your own image. `steps/before-plan`, `steps/stop`, `steps/gate`, `init/image` |
| 13 | Private module registry | 3 | a monorepo-friendly Terraform and OpenTofu registry | each release is written as the module registry protocol to your bucket, with namespaces by tag prefix and `tofu test` before release ([guide](/terragucci/guides/publish-modules/#serve-a-module-registry)). `modules/registry`, `modules/publish` |
| 14 | Cost estimation | 3 | cost estimates on each pull request | Infracost figures in the plan note, in the policy input, and as a threshold that holds a wave at its gate ([guide](/terragucci/guides/estimate-cost/)). `cost/estimate`, `cost/gate`, `cost/policy` |
| 15 | Policy as code | 3 | portable OPA and Rego on plans | conftest or OPA over each plan, HCP Terraform policy sets and `policies.hcl`, from the repo or a shared source at a pinned ref ([reference](/terragucci/reference/policy/)). `policy/opa`, `policy/hcl`, `policy/source` |
| 16 | AI: trusted PR review | 3 | a model reviews a change's intent against its plan, from default-branch instructions, on your own model | `review.agent` runs your model on the description, diff and plan with instructions from the default branch and no forge token; the note approves nothing ([guide](/terragucci/guides/agent-review-a-pull-request/)). `agent/review`, `policy/review-risk` |
| 17 | Collaboration integration | 4 | interactive approvals in Slack and Teams | `terragucci relay`, in your cloud, records a Slack click or a Teams reply as the approver's own approval; drift notices carry a re-plan button ([guide](/terragucci/guides/approve-from-chat/)). `chat/slack`, `chat/teams`, `chat/replan` |
| 18 | VCS integration | 3 | plan comments and checks, merge gating, operations from comments | a plan note and status on each pull request, comment commands for plan and apply, on GitHub, GitLab and Forgejo ([guide](/terragucci/guides/re-plan-from-a-comment/)). Bitbucket and Azure DevOps get no pipeline. `comment/plan`, `comment/apply`, `security/fork` |
| 19 | Observability | 3 | run events, metrics and audit to Datadog, OTel or webhooks | OpenTelemetry traces and metrics to any OTLP endpoint ([guide](/terragucci/guides/send-traces-and-metrics/)), signed webhooks ([event](/terragucci/reference/notify-event/)) and an [audit trail](/terragucci/reference/audit-trail/). `telemetry/traces`, `telemetry/metrics`, `notify/webhook`, `audit/tofu` |
| 20 | Visualizations / graphs | 2 | resource and dependency graphs, deployment views | the estate page draws roots by wave with cross-project edges; the run view shows blast radius and a timeline ([guide](/terragucci/guides/see-every-project/)). `estate/graph` |
| 21 | AI: agentic ops & MCP | 2 | an MCP server, state context, agents that act on drift | [`terragucci mcp`](/terragucci/guides/agent-read-over-mcp/) serves the estate read-only, and the [drift agent](/terragucci/guides/agent-fix-drift/) opens a pull request that plans like any other. `estate/mcp`, `agent/drift` |
| 22 | Ephemeral environments | 1 | short-lived environments with a TTL | a copy of the named roots per pull request, destroyed through a planned destroy on close or when its TTL passes ([guide](/terragucci/guides/ephemeral-environments/)). `ephemeral/pr`, `ephemeral/ttl` |
| 23 | Terraform provider | 1 | manage the platform through Terraform | the terragucci provider's data sources give Terraform each environment's cloud trust and the live setup check ([guide](/terragucci/guides/manage-the-control-repo-with-terraform/)). You build it from the repository; its publication to the OpenTofu and Terraform registries is pending ([#659](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/terragucci/issues/659)). |
| 24 | Multi-cloud support | 1 | clouds beyond AWS | OIDC to AWS, GCP and Azure, and reports in S3, GCS or Azure Blob Storage ([guide](/terragucci/guides/keep-reports-in-a-bucket/)). `oidc/clouds`, `report/gcs`, `report/azure` |

## Gates

tacos.guru also lists five pass or fail checks.

| Gate | Checks | terragucci |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | no Kubernetes required | jobs in your CI; nothing to install in a cluster |
| G2 | self-hosted runners in your cloud account | your CI's runners, wherever you run them |
| G3 | a CDK Terrain synth step before plan | the `synth` key ([guide](/terragucci/guides/plan-cdk-terrain-stacks/)). `init/cdktn-synth` |
| G4 | actively maintained: recent releases, at least three active committers, the core team still on it | a judgement of the maintainers, not a feature, so this page makes no claim for it. The [releases](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/terragucci/releases) and [commits](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/terragucci/commits/main) are public |
| G5 | OpenTofu support (advisory) | the default binary. `init/root-pins` |
