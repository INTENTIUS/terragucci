## The lifecycle around your roots

Welcome, Terraform users. terragucci carries a root through review, rollout and the watch that follows, and you write none of the pipeline.

<Lifecycle />

## One note for two hundred plans

A change to a shared module can touch hundreds of roots. Your reviewer gets one note on the pull request that groups the roots taking the same change. Every destroy and every replacement is still named.

<PlanNote />

## Rollouts in waves

The change goes out a few roots at a time. Each wave waits for its own approval, and that approval covers exactly the plans it was given.

<Waves />

## Keep what you have

terragucci reads your roots and runs your binary. It adds the steps around them and changes nothing inside them.

## Start with one root

Point terragucci at a root and choose your forge. Your next pull request gets a plan note, and your next merge goes out in waves.
